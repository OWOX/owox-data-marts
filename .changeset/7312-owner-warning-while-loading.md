**Owners no longer look removed from the project while a page loads**

The owners of a Data Mart, Storage, Destination, Report or Credential are listed on its page, and a yellow warning marks an owner who has left the project. Previously that warning showed next to every owner for about a second each time the page opened, because the list of project members had not loaded yet. Anyone checking who owns a resource could not tell whether an owner had really left. If the member list failed to load, the warning stayed. Now the warning appears only after the member list has loaded, and only next to owners who are no longer members of the project.

See [Ownership](../../docs/project/ownership-and-sharing.md#ownership).
