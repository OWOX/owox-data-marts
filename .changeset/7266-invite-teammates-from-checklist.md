**Fewer steps to invite teammates from the setup checklist**

When you set up a new project and want colleagues to join, the **Invite teammates** item in the **Get to know OWOX** checklist used to open a panel with an **Invite teammates** step, and inside it an **Invite teammates** button: three clicks on the same words before you reached the member list. Now the checklist item opens **Project Settings → Members** right away, where **Invite member** sends the invitation.

The item is still checked off once a teammate is invited. See [Invite a Member](../../docs/project/members.md#invite-a-member).
