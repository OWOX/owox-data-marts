**Find the right help from your project or profile**

When you manage [project settings or your profile](../../docs/project/), check
[credit consumption](../../docs/getting-started/billing/consumption-units.md), or
[request project access](../../docs/project/members.md#manual-provisioning), you can open the relevant
guide directly from the screen. In OWOX Cloud, the account menu also links to the Privacy Policy and
Terms of Service.
