**Clearer canvas settings on the Models canvas**

The gear menu on **Data Marts → Models** is regrouped, and every checkbox now hides exactly what it names.

- **Card content** has a checkbox for each part of a card: Input source, Field count, Triggers, Reports, Relationships, Draft badge, and Quality and sharing. Before, the trigger, report and relationship counts had no checkbox of their own and stayed on the card.
- **Field rows** holds Field aliases and Field descriptions. It appears only in the Detailed view, where field rows are shown.
- Ticking or unticking a checkbox, or the join fields switch, keeps your zoom and position on the canvas. Picking another view or layout algorithm still fits the whole graph.
- **Check all** and **Uncheck all** are gone. Untick every Card content checkbox to leave only the titles. A title-only preference saved earlier still shows only the titles.
- The menu scrolls when the window is too short to fit it.

See [Models Canvas](../../docs/getting-started/setup-guide/models-canvas.md#canvas-settings).
