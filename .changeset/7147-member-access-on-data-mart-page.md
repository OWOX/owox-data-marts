**Faster to fix access: see who can work with a Data Mart, and why**

When a member couldn't find a Data Mart in the catalog or through MCP, a Project Admin had to compare the member's role and contexts in Project settings with the Data Mart's owners, sharing and contexts on another screen, and work out the answer by hand. Now the Data Mart's **Overview** tab lists every project member with what they can do with it, how they got that access, and what to change to give them more — for example, _To see it: add Paid ads in Contexts_.

- Each member's row shows their role, what their access comes through — Ownership, _Shared for reporting_, _Shared for maintenance_, or a shared context — and what they can do: see the Data Mart and build reports on it, edit it, or change its Sharing and Ownership.
- The **Sharing** and **Contexts** sections warn how many members a setting keeps out, such as a Data Mart without a context that members limited to selected contexts can't see.
- The list follows the rules the product enforces and is visible to Project Admins only; see [Ownership and Sharing](../../docs/project/ownership-and-sharing.md#see-who-can-work-with-a-data-mart).
