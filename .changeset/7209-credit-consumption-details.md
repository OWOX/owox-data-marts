**Find what used your project credits**

When reviewing a month's credit usage, you can now open an operation inline, search by source name or ID, and use filters to narrow down the details. Follow links to Data Marts, Google Sheets documents, and individual sheets to identify what used the credits. See [consumption units](../../docs/getting-started/billing/consumption-units.md) for what each operation measures.

Projects without a credit allowance show consumption only. Subscription shows the actual status of internal plans and links to Stripe for eligible billing accounts. Billing help is available from the Subscription details header.
