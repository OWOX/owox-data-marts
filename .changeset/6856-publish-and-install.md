**Install a plugin as you publish it**

The **Publish a plugin** sheet now publishes and installs in one step. **Publish & Install** lists the plugin, asks you to confirm the installation, and opens the plugin, so it is in your menu right away. Previously a published plugin was missing from your menu until you found it in the Gallery and installed it.

- **Publish only**, in the button's menu, lists the plugin without installing it.
- **Publish & Install** on a plugin you already have opens it.

See [Publish in OWOX Data Marts](../../docs/plugins/authoring-guide.md#publish-in-owox-data-marts).
