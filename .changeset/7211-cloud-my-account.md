**Manage your Cloud profile from Request access**

While waiting for project access, you can manage your personal details in OWOX Data Marts. Open **My profile** from the user menu to check your sign-in email and edit your profile.

You can also request deletion of your Cloud account and cancel the request while it is pending.

The **Projects** table shows your memberships and roles. Search by project name or filter by role or project status.

Use **Email preferences** to manage your personal service notifications, tips and suggestions, and offers.

See [My profile in OWOX Cloud](../../docs/project/index.md#my-profile-in-owox-cloud).
