**Faster plugin updates: check for a new version from the Plugins page**

You publish a plugin release and want it now, not after the daily update check. Previously, you opened each plugin's page, clicked **Check and Update** and went back to **Plugins**. Now the card of each installed plugin on the **Plugins** page has **Check and Update** next to its version. The check runs in place, a message tells you the result, and the card shows the new version.

![The Plugins page with two installed plugin cards, the Check and Update tooltip over the button next to the Gyroscope version, and the message "You're up to date — v0.6.0"](https://imagedelivery.net/zKr-4bdC5CBGL2DuuEmvYw/c963279f-7ac9-42f8-d35c-b739f31ab800/public)

See [Update or roll back](../../docs/plugins/authoring-guide.md#update-or-roll-back).
