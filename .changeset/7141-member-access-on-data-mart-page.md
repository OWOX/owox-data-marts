**Faster to fix access: see who can work with a Data Mart, and why**

When a member couldn't find a Data Mart in the catalog or through MCP, a Project Admin had to compare the member's role and contexts in Project settings with the Data Mart's owners, sharing and contexts on another screen, and work out the answer by hand. Now the Data Mart's **Overview** tab lists every project member with what they can do with it, how they got that access, and what to change to give them more — for example, _Add one of their contexts to this Data Mart_.

- **Use** means seeing the Data Mart in the catalog and through MCP and building reports on it, **Maintain** means editing, deleting and managing triggers, and **Manage access** means changing sharing and owners.
- The **Sharing** and **Contexts** sections warn how many members a setting keeps out, such as a Data Mart without a context that members limited to selected contexts can't see.
- The list follows the rules the product enforces and is visible to Project Admins only; see [Ownership and Sharing](../../docs/project/ownership-and-sharing.md#see-who-can-work-with-a-data-mart).
