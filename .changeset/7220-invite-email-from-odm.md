**Invited teammates get their email right away, with the role they will have**

When a Project Admin invited a teammate, the invitation email went through a separate mailing service. It could arrive minutes later, named the role as `ADMIN`, `EDITOR` or `VIEWER` instead of the names you see in the product, and if it was never delivered, the admin did not know. Now OWOX Data Marts sends the invitation itself as soon as you click **Send invitation**.

- The email names the project, who invited the person, and their role: Project Admin, Data Owner or Business User. Its **Accept invitation** button opens the project.
- If the email cannot be sent after a few attempts, the invitation still stands, and you see a message asking you to tell the person yourself.
- Only OWOX-managed projects send this email. Self-hosted installations keep the one-time invitation link.

See [Invite a Member](../../docs/project/members.md#invite-a-member).
